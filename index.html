回忆地球隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月 26 日 · English

照片和本地记录

你可以允许访问所有照片、仅选择部分照片，或拒绝访问。应用读取获准照片的系统标识、拍摄日期和已有坐标，在设备上整理相册。手动回忆、隐藏状态、补充的地点、照片分组和已查询的地名保存在应用数据中。两个引导标记记录你是否看过首开介绍和操作提示。补地点和隐藏只影响应用内的显示。开发者不接收照片或回忆记录，应用没有账号、广告或跟踪服务。

何时使用网络

首页用随包国界在本机归组。进入地区或“地名整理中”相册时，应用可将待解析坐标交给 Apple 的系统地名服务，获取国家、省市等名称并缓存在本机。选点地图使用 Apple 地图。显示缩略图或浏览照片时，如果原图在 iCloud 照片中，系统可从你的 iCloud 照片载入所需图像。

撤回授权和删除

在 iPhone 的“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → 回忆地球”更改授权范围。失去授权的照片在相册刷新后从应用中消失。手动回忆和地点分配可以保留，供重新授权时使用。要移除本机全部应用记录，请在“设置 → 通用 → iPhone 储存空间 → 回忆地球”选择“删除 App”。系统相册中的照片会保留。

本政策托管在 GitHub Pages，GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址。隐私问题可通过政策仓库联系开发者。公开留言请勿附上照片、坐标或其他私人资料。

Memory Earth Privacy Policy

Updated November 26, 2026

Photos and local records

You can allow full or selected photo access, or decline access. The app reads authorized photos’ system identifiers, capture dates and available coordinates to organize albums on your device. App storage holds journal entries, hidden states, assigned locations, groupings and cached place names. Two preferences remember whether you have seen the introduction and gesture hint. Hiding a memory or assigning a location affects only the app. The developer does not receive your photos or journal. The app has no accounts, advertising or tracking services.

Network use

The home screen groups photos using country boundaries bundled with the app. Opening a region or the pending-place album can send unresolved coordinates to Apple’s system geocoding service for administrative names. Results are cached locally. The location picker uses Apple Maps. When displaying thumbnails or photos, the system may load images from your iCloud Photos library.

Access and deletion

Change access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Memory Earth. Photos outside the authorized selection disappear after refresh. Manual entries and location assignments can remain available for future access. To erase local app records, choose Delete App in Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Memory Earth. Your system photo library is preserved.

GitHub Pages hosts this policy. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the policy repository. Please keep photos, coordinates and personal details out of public messages.
